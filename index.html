Curious about the technical details?

How access works. You use your domain registrar's (or DNS host's) own sharing settings to give us scoped, temporary access to your DNS records only. It is not a login to your account, and we remove it when the job is done. If your domain points to a hosting platform's name servers, we work through that platform instead.

What we configure. We create your Google Workspace account, add the MX records that route your mail to Google, and publish SPF (which servers may send as you), DKIM (a digital signature on your outgoing mail) and DMARC (what receivers should do with mail that fails those checks).

Why it matters. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are what keep your invoices and replies out of spam and make it harder for someone to impersonate your domain. We finish with a full delivery test.

FAQ

Common questions

Still on the fence? Here's what most people ask before getting started.

Do I need to already own a domain?+

No, but you'll need to get one before we can start — it only takes about 5 minutes and typically costs $12–18/year through any registrar. A few simple, reputable options: Namecheap (our usual recommendation — simple pricing, easy to use), GoDaddy, or Squarespace Domains. Not sure whether you already have one? Put your website or business address on the intake form and we'll look it up for you. Once you own one, we'll handle everything from there.

When do I pay?+

After your email is working. We send an invoice for the flat setup fee once you've received your handoff document. Google's own Workspace subscription (~$7/month) is separate and billed to you by Google.

What is Google Workspace?+

Google Workspace is Google's paid business suite (~$7/month) that lets you use Gmail with your own custom domain. It includes Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Meet, and more — all under your brand.

What if I already use Gmail for business?+

No problem. We can set up forwarding from your old Gmail address so you don't miss any emails during the transition. Most clients run both for a week or two, then fully switch over.

How do I know my account stays secure?+

We use Google's own secure sharing tools, so we never ask for or store your password. Once setup is complete and confirmed, we remove our temporary access entirely. Your account is 100% yours.

Can I get multiple email addresses?+

Yes. Alias addresses like info@, billing@, and support@ are included at no extra charge. They all route to your main inbox — no additional mailboxes or fees.

What's included in the 30-day support?+

Any questions about your new email, help setting it up on additional devices, or minor configuration adjustments. Reach us by phone, text, or email — we respond same day.
